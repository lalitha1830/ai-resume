About

What Is ResumeAI?

ResumeAI is an open-source portfolio project that demonstrates how modern generative AI can help job seekers understand their resume's strengths and weaknesses relative to a specific job description. It is powered by Google Gemini and built with Python and Flask. ResumeAI does not replace professional career advice — it is a tool to help you ask better questions about your own resume.

{# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════ PRIVACY SECTION ═══════════════════════════════════════════════ #}

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

When you upload a resume, ResumeAI extracts the text and sends it to the Google Gemini API to perform the analysis. The uploaded file is read into memory and is not stored on our servers. Only the analysis results are saved to your account.

Please avoid uploading documents that contain unnecessary sensitive personal information such as national ID numbers, financial details, or medical information. A resume should contain only what you would comfortably share with a prospective employer.
